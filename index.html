What to actually control

Five things · everything else can stay as it is
  1. Roti and rice portionsThe biggest hidden calorie load. Keep lunch and dinner to 2–3 roti instead of 3–4, and fill the rest of your hunger with dal and sabzi.
  2. Oil and ghee at homeHome sabzi usually carries more oil than you'd guess. Ask for less — same taste, fewer calories.
  3. Sugar in your two teasHalf a spoon or less. Two cups a day adds up over a month.
  4. Fried snacks like bhujiyaOnce or twice a week is fine. It's a frequency problem, not a ban.
  5. Dinner size specificallyLittle activity follows it. Keep the carbs lighter at night and let protein and vegetables fill the plate.
Eat these without counting

Dal, sabzi, paneer, soya, eggs, oats, curd, salad, fruit. All of it works with the plan. Trying to restrict everything is what makes people quit in week two.

When results actually show

  • 2–3 weeksNothing visible yet. Energy improves and your push-up and pull-up numbers start moving. This is the phase that tests you.
  • 4–6 weeksShirts sit differently around the waist and shoulders. Push-up reps climb noticeably.
  • 8–10 weeksMirror changes begin — some definition in shoulders and arms, waist tightening.
  • 3–4 monthsThe physique reads as "in shape" to other people. This is the payoff point.
  • 6 monthsThe lean, balanced look you described — assuming you held roughly 80% consistency.

Three non-negotiables regardless of how flexible the food gets: 7–8 hours of sleep, four gym days a week, and protein roughly on target most days.
